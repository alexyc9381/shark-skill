# Ready-made pitches

Paste any of these into Claude Code after installing the skill. The more you fill in, the harder the
investors can push. Whatever you leave out becomes your homework list.

1. **A product idea:** `/shark [product] for [customer], priced at [price]. It costs me [cost] to make. [how many] people have bought it so far.`
2. **A side hustle:** `/shark I want to turn [side hustle] into a business. I make [income] a month from it today, working [hours] a week.`
3. **An app or SaaS:** `/shark [app] helps [customer] do [job]. [users] people use it, [paying] pay [price] a month. I need [amount] for [use].`
4. **A service or agency:** `/shark I want to sell [service] to [customer] for [price]. I have [clients] clients so far.`
5. **A creator business:** `/shark I have [followers] followers on [platform] and I want to sell [product] to them for [price].`
6. **Before a real investor meeting:** `/shark [paste your actual pitch deck text or one-pager]`

Want a tougher room? Add `--investors 10`.
