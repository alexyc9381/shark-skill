# No deal: all 2 sharks are out

**The pitch:** A subscription box of hot sauces from small local makers, $35 a month.

## The sharks

| Shark | Decision | Offer | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Operator Shark | OUT | none | I'm out because the answers gave me nothing on who packs, whether the makers can supply 500 boxes a month, or whether anyone stays, and I won't put $50,000 into bulk stock and ads for a business I can't see inside. |
| The Customer Shark | OUT | none | A bottle of hot sauce lasts me weeks, so I would cancel once my shelf is full, and the founder could not say how long any of the 12 have stayed or how many have quit, so nothing shows anyone keeps paying $35 a month. |

## What would change their minds

- **The Operator Shark:** A one-page costing of a 500-box month with a written quote behind every line (bottles from named makers who agree to that volume, packaging, postage, and the packer's time), ending in a cost per box I can hold against the $19.
- **The Customer Shark:** A dated list of everyone who has ever subscribed, including anyone who cancelled, that shows most of them still paying after their third box.

## Your homework: questions you could not answer

- The founder needs to find out who packs and ships today, whether anyone's time is inside the $19, and what the $19 splits into (sauce, packaging, postage). Also find out what each part would cost at 500 boxes a month.
- The founder needs to find out which makers will supply 500 boxes' worth a month, and at what price per bottle. The pitch names no maker. It also gives no split of the $50,000, only that it is "to buy stock in bulk and run ads." Find out the dollar split between stock and ads, and what happens to unsold stock if growth stalls.
- The founder needs to find out how many of the 12 still pay, how many months each has stayed, and what one ad-won subscriber costs. All 12 came from posting on Instagram, so the pitch shows no ad-won subscriber.
- The founder needs to find out each of the 12's sign-up date, how many people have ever subscribed, and how many cancelled. The pitch says "12 paying subscribers so far" but not whether that means 12 now or 12 ever.
- The founder needs to find out what each maker charges for the same bottles direct, and what the box offers that going direct does not. Also ask each of the 12 what they spent less on to afford $35 a month.
- The founder needs to find out why each of the 12 says they subscribed, and whether they knew the founder before buying. The pitch says only that all 12 came from posting on Instagram. It has no ad results, so nothing shows whether a stranger reached by an ad would subscribe or stay.
