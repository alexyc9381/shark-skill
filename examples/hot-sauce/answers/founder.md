# Founder answers

## Investor 1

**1. Packing, cost, and 500 boxes**
The pitch says a box costs "about $19 to fill and ship." At that figure, 500 boxes a month cost about $9,500 ($19 x 500).
NOT ANSWERED: the founder needs to find out who packs and ships today, whether anyone's time is inside the $19, and what the $19 splits into (sauce, packaging, postage). Also find out what each part would cost at 500 boxes a month.

**2. Maker supply and the $50,000**
NOT ANSWERED: the founder needs to find out which makers will supply 500 boxes' worth a month, and at what price per bottle. The pitch names no maker. It also gives no split of the $50,000, only that it is "to buy stock in bulk and run ads." Find out the dollar split between stock and ads, and what happens to unsold stock if growth stalls.

**3. Ad cost limit and retention**
Yes: $35 minus $19 leaves $16 per box per month, before ads. The most an ad can cost per subscriber is $16 times the months that person stays. The pitch gives neither months nor ad cost.
NOT ANSWERED: the founder needs to find out how many of the 12 still pay, how many months each has stayed, and what one ad-won subscriber costs. All 12 came from posting on Instagram, so the pitch shows no ad-won subscriber.

## Investor 2

**1. Longest stay and cancellations**
NOT ANSWERED: the founder needs to find out each of the 12's sign-up date, how many people have ever subscribed, and how many cancelled. The pitch says "12 paying subscribers so far" but not whether that means 12 now or 12 ever.

**2. Buying direct, and what they gave up**
The pitch gives only the product: hot sauces from small local makers, $35 a month. It gives no reason to choose the box over buying direct.
NOT ANSWERED: the founder needs to find out what each maker charges for the same bottles direct, and what the box offers that going direct does not. Also ask each of the 12 what they spent less on to afford $35 a month.

**3. Why they subscribed, and ad strangers**
NOT ANSWERED: the founder needs to find out why each of the 12 says they subscribed, and whether they knew the founder before buying. The pitch says only that all 12 came from posting on Instagram. It has no ad results, so nothing shows whether a stranger reached by an ad would subscribe or stay.
